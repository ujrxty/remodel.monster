## Our Mission

This website is a free service dedicated to assisting homeowners in connecting with local service providers for their home improvement needs. We understand that finding reliable, qualified contractors can be challenging, and our platform aims to simplify this process by facilitating connections between homeowners and professionals in their area.

## How Our Service Works

Our platform serves as a bridge between homeowners seeking home improvement services and local contractors and service providers. When you submit your project details through our form, we connect you with relevant professionals who may be able to assist with your specific needs.

The connection process is straightforward:
- Homeowners provide details about their project requirements
- We match these requirements with appropriate service providers
- Qualified professionals are connected with potential clients
- Direct communication begins between homeowners and contractors

## Independent Service Providers

**Important:** All contractors and service providers connected through our platform are independent businesses. This website does not employ, endorse, or directly control any of the contractors or service providers. Each professional operates their own business, sets their own rates, and manages their own scheduling and service delivery.

We do not warrant or guarantee any work performed by contractors connected through our service. The quality, timeliness, and satisfaction of any work performed is solely between the homeowner and the chosen contractor.

## Homeowner Responsibilities

As a homeowner using our connection service, you maintain full responsibility for:

- **Verification of Credentials:** It is your responsibility to verify that any hired contractor furnishes the necessary licenses and insurance required for the work being performed in your area
- **Due Diligence:** Conducting appropriate research on any contractor before hiring, including checking references, reviews, and credentials
- **Contract Negotiations:** All agreements, pricing, timelines, and contract terms are between you and the contractor
- **Project Management:** Overseeing and managing any work performed on your property
